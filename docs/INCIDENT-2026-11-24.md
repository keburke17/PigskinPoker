# 2026-11-24: Week 12 finalized before it was dealt, and Week 13 dealt in its place

Written for Kyle, who owns the hosted side and runs the repair. Scott has read the
summary.

## What the league saw

On Tuesday morning a season league with the clock switched on got a deal email for
**Week 13**. Week 12 was never dealt. It shows as finished, with every team on 0 points,
one team credited with the week win, and the full standings ladder handed out by the
tiebreakers. The league says it is on Week 13; the NFL is on Week 12.

## Why

`finalizeIfReady` (server/operations.js) did this:

1. read the league and check that Week 11 may be finalized (`advanceEligibility`);
2. download games.csv (8.6 MB) and run one last stats pull - several seconds;
3. **re-read the league and finalize whatever week was current by then.**

If Week 11 was finalized by something else during step 2, the re-read in step 3 finds
an undealt Week 12 in `pre-deal`, and the clock finalizes that:

- the engine's `finalizeCurrentPeriod` has no phase rule of its own - the commissioner's
  button is protected by `guard`, the clock was not;
- the Nov 10 claim in `runLifecycle` claims the version and phase *of the row just
  re-read*, so it accepted `pre-deal` as happily as anything else.

Empty rosters score 0, `rankTeamsWithTiebreak` still orders them, and the standings
ladder pays out N..1. `mapNewPeriod` then mapped the new Week 13 to NFL week 13, and the
same run's deal step dealt it against NFL week 13's schedule.

**The "something else"** was either the commissioner pressing Finalize on Week 11 by hand
on Tuesday morning, or an overlapping hourly run. The activity log tells them apart: an
automatic finalize says "finalized automatically". The bogus line for Week 12 will claim
"every game of NFL week 12 was final" - false, which is the tell. If it was two runs,
the Netlify function log will show two `[run-cycle]` lines for the same hour, and that is
worth knowing for its own sake: overlapping runs are also behind the table deals below.

### The table deal mismatches since Nov 10 are the same bug

`dealIfDue` has the same shape: check `pre-deal`, refresh the pool (slow), re-read, deal.
A second run that re-read the week after the first had dealt it found `dealt`, claimed
that, and dealt again. The managers had been emailed the first roster (the notifications
log sends once per person per week) and the game kept the second. The Nov 10 fix stopped
two writes landing at once; it did not stop a run acting on a week that changed under it.

## The fix (this branch)

- `samePeriodStill`: after its slow reads, the clock acts only if the re-read week is the
  one it checked, still in a phase it may act on. Otherwise the step is a skip ("another
  run got to this week first") and the deal step re-reads before deciding.
- `wrongPhase`: deal, process schemes and finalize each refuse to run from the wrong phase,
  whoever calls them.
- `finalized_at` is carried forward through blob writes (server/league.js). It was being
  wiped from every finished week on the next write, and `dealWindowOpen` reads it - a
  Monday-night finalize followed by any edit meant no Tuesday deal. Found on the way; not
  the cause here.
- OQ-40, Scott: weeks with a Wednesday game close schemes at 3am Wednesday.

Tests: three regressions in tests/server.test.js (the finalize race, the deal race,
`finalized_at`) fail on the old code and pass on the new.

## The repair (hosted database - Kyle)

There is no button that un-finalizes a week, so it is a script. Dry run by default; it
refuses unless the league looks exactly like the incident (current week N+1, Week N
finalized, never dealt, every score 0).

```bash
npm run db:backup
SUPABASE_URL=https://<ref>.supabase.co SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY=sb_secret_... \
  node scripts/repair-skipped-week.mjs --league <league uuid> --week 12          # look
SUPABASE_URL=... SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY=... \
  node scripts/repair-skipped-week.mjs --league <league uuid> --week 12 --apply  # do it
```

It removes Week 12's results and takes them back out of every team's totals, deletes Week
13 (rosters, schemes, its deal-email row - all cascade), puts Week 12 back to `pre-deal`
with its NFL week kept and its finalize/deal stamps cleared, and logs a line saying so.
Week 12's recap lines stay marked used in `seasons.recap_deck`; harmless.

Then the commissioner presses **Deal** for Week 12. That re-reads Week 12's schedule and
emails every manager. If the clock gets there first in the next hourly run, that is the
same deal.
